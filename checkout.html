Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Sec+ to enroll?
Yes — for the Cohort and Cohort+Coaching tiers. This program assumes Sec+ knowledge and picks up where Professor Messer's free Sec+ training stops. If you haven't passed Sec+ yet, finish Messer's free training and the exam first, then come back. The Self-Guided tier doesn't require Sec+, but you'll get more out of it if you have it.
When does the next cohort start?
Cohorts run on a rolling basis as seats fill. Reserve your seat and you'll be locked into the next available start date. We send the schedule and kickoff call link the same day your seat is confirmed.
What's the difference between the three tiers?
Self-Guided ($497) is content-only — all 12 modules, the lab environment, AI Tutor, and lifetime access. Cohort ($1,497) adds 12 live weekly calls, peer pods, dual-track resume, mock interviews, warm intros, and the 60-day post-cohort coaching window. Cohort + Coaching ($1,997) adds 4 one-on-one coaching calls plus direct Slack/Discord access to the founder for 90 days post-graduation.
What's the refund policy?
Self-Guided: 30-day money-back guarantee from the date of purchase. Cohort and Cohort+Coaching: 14-day refund from your cohort's start date — full refund, no questions asked. Email info@cybersecproacademy.org.
Do you offer a job guarantee?
No — and we don't pretend to. Hiring is on the other side of the table. What we do offer is the 60-day post-cohort coaching window, mock interviews, dual-track resume work, LinkedIn rewrite, and warm intros to hiring managers in our network where openings exist. The work you do controls the outcome.
How long do I have access to the content?
Self-Guided: Lifetime access to content and updates. Cohort tiers: Full lab/content access through your cohort plus the 60-day post-cohort coaching window — that's roughly 5 months of active access from kickoff.
Can I switch tiers after enrolling?
Yes, before your cohort starts. Email info@cybersecproacademy.org with your order number and we'll handle the upgrade or downgrade.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover) and PayPal. Checkout is processed securely by Teachable — your card details are encrypted and never touch our servers.
